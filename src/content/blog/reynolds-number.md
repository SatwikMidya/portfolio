---
title: "Reynolds Number in Bird Flight"
description: "Understanding low Reynolds number aerodynamics in birds"
date: "2025-03-12"
---

The Reynolds number is defined as:

$$
Re = \frac{\rho V L}{\mu}
$$

For birds, the Reynolds number typically lies in the range
$10^4$–$10^6$, which leads to laminar or transitional flow regimes.

This has important implications for lift generation and efficiency.
